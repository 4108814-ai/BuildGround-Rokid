# Product context

## Register

Product.

## Users

Rokid AR glasses owners and developers who want useful phone-powered features on the glasses without repeatedly installing or managing software on the wearable itself.

## Product purpose

Rokid Nexus is a plugin platform with one permanent glasses hub and isolated phone-side plugins. It keeps installation, configuration, computation, consent, and network work on the phone while presenting concise, glanceable surfaces on the glasses.

## Brand personality

Focused, technical, trustworthy, and quietly capable.

## Anti-references

- Decorative or novelty-heavy wearable interfaces.
- Opaque security consent or hidden capability grants.
- Unstable layouts, unnecessary motion, and periodic visual churn.
- Phone-sized interaction patterns transplanted onto the glasses HUD.

## Design principles

- Keep state and controls stable while background work happens.
- Make trust boundaries, capability grants, and destructive actions explicit.
- Put complex configuration on the phone; keep the glasses HUD sparse and glanceable.
- Design for real device constraints, intermittent links, and recoverable failures.
- Prefer clear status, direct actions, and predictable navigation over decoration.

## Accessibility and inclusion

Use readable type, strong contrast, generous touch targets on the phone, and one-axis DPAD or touchpad navigation on the glasses. Never depend on color, touch, or motion alone to communicate state. Avoid unnecessary animation and preserve the user's scroll and focus position during background refreshes.
