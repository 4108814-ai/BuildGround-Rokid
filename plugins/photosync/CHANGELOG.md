# Changelog — Photo Sync

## 1.0.0 — unreleased

- First release: glasses captures copy themselves into `Download/Hi Rokid/` on
  the phone when the glasses start charging, when the phone reconnects while they
  are charging, or on demand.
- Settings screen with live status and progress, a *Sync now* button, the recent
  sync history, and the two switches: sync-when-charging (on) and
  delete-from-glasses-after-sync (off, with the honest warning when the glasses
  refuse to delete).
