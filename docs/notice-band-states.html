The notice band — six states

Colors, sizes, and borders are the renderer's own (phosphor #71FF97, band at 92% of the optic's width, pure black fills for the additive optics). A notice lives 2 to 45 seconds — or until the reader stops turning pages — and BACK dismisses it in every state.

1

Plain band

Neither interactive nor actions. It says its piece and leaves on its TTL. It listens to nothing: every gesture passes through to whatever is underneath.

Answered

A notice takes exactly one answer, of either kind. The moment it is given, the row leaves the band, nothing is claimed, and nothing fires again. The TTL runs on unchanged — answering buys no time.

5

Paged

A body longer than one page holds. It carries no actions, and that is the rule rather than a coincidence: a notice is paged or answerable, never both, so forward and backward never have two meanings at once. The glasses decide how many pages this is — they are the only side that has measured how wide a line runs.

Long notice

This band carries more text than a single page can hold, which is the whole point of it: a relayed message is as long as whoever wrote it decided, and a tier that only ever showed the first four lines was not relaying anything.

6

With an image

The picture travels inside the notice/show envelope itself, so the band cannot appear before it — they are the same message, and there is no half-arrived state to design around. It draws on the first page only; a picture is worth the first screen, not a tax on every one.
